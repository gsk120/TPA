The calendar worker runs inside the bot process.
Use "mode paused" to pause calendar sync while keeping the bot running.
Omit APPROVAL on unlink or undo-import to preview the impact.
Use --private-title to hide the Google event title in TPA.
Use review --skip-initial to hide initial_review items.
After detachment, use preview for a fresh approval and import, import-read-only, or link to reconnect.
